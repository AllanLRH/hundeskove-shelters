What leaves your browser

This page is static: the shelter and dog-forest data is generated ahead of time and served as files, so browsing, filtering and picking dates send nothing anywhere.

The one exception is the address you type for driving times. When you submit it — never while you type — the address text is sent to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) to turn it into a coordinate, and that coordinate is then sent to OSRM (FOSSGIS e.V., Germany) to measure driving times. Both are OpenStreetMap’s own public services, and both have their own privacy terms.

Your address is stored only in this browser’s localStorage, on your own machine. It is never sent to this site’s server and is deliberately kept out of the shareable link in the address bar, so a link you share carries your filters but not where you live. Clearing the address field removes it. Map tiles are fetched from openstreetmap.org as you pan, which reveals your IP address to them in the ordinary way any image request does.
